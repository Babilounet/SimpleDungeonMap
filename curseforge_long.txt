SimpleDungeonMap displays dungeon map overlays directly on the World Map for WoW Classic Anniversary Edition. Open your map inside any Classic or TBC dungeon and see the full layout as an overlay.

Based on DungeonMaps 0.1c by Undeadguy, fully refactored for Anniversary Edition (Interface 20505).

## Features

**Dungeon Map Overlay**
Open the World Map inside a dungeon and the map overlay appears automatically. Right-click to dismiss and return to the normal map.

**Floor Switching**
Multi-floor dungeons show numbered buttons in the top-left corner. Click to switch floors. The current floor is highlighted. Floors auto-switch based on your subzone as you move through the dungeon.

**Portal Click Preview**
With Leatrix Maps installed, click on the blue dungeon portal icons on the World Map to preview any dungeon map without being inside. Works for all zones with dungeon entrances.

**Questie Quest Pins**
With Questie installed, toggle the ? button in the top-right corner to display quest objective pins directly on the dungeon overlay. Shows kill targets, objects, loot sources, and turn-in NPCs for your active quests.

**TBC (16):** Hellfire Ramparts, The Blood Furnace, The Shattered Halls, The Slave Pens, The Underbog, The Steamvault, Mana-Tombs, Auchenai Crypts, Sethekk Halls, Shadow Labyrinth, The Mechanar, The Botanica, The Arcatraz, Old Hillsbrad Foothills, The Black Morass, Magisters' Terrace

## Requirements

BLP texture files must be provided separately. The addon expects tiled dungeon map textures in Interface\Worldmap\{DungeonName}\ (12 tiles per floor, 4x3 grid of 256x256). Extract from game data using CASCExplorer or similar tools.

## Optional Dependencies

- **Leatrix Maps** - for portal click preview on the world map
- **Questie** - for quest objective pins on the dungeon overlay

## Commands

- /sdm - show current dungeon info
- /sdm questpins - toggle quest pins on/off
- /sdm debug - debug info

## Notes

- Works with any client locale (uses numeric instance IDs, not dungeon names)
- Compatible with Leatrix Maps (uses HookScript, no conflicts)
- Settings persist between sessions
